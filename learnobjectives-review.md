## Findings the panel converged on

- **Chapter 12 (Midnight) is the unanimous floor** — every persona scored it lowest or near-lowest (3.5–6.0): the Zswap protocol named in the objectives never appears in the chapter (grep-verified: it appears only in chapters 2, 3, and 7), nullifier treatment is thin, the author's own IOG/Midnight position goes unexamined, and vendor documentation is cited as verification.
- **The 4×4 Sudoku thread thins after chapter 6** and vanishes from chapters 7, 8, 11, and 13 (educator), weakening the book's own signature pedagogical device exactly where the material gets hardest.
- **Narration where rigor was demanded** (cryptographer, 6.2 mean): the sum-check soundness proof, the ZK simulator, and every security game are absent — the book consistently teaches *about* the theorems rather than the theorems.
- **Strong operational grounding in the core craft chapters** (engineer: 8.3 on chapters 5–6) but whitepaper-level treatment elsewhere; the Airbender field ambiguity the graph flagged resolves to an **unsourced in-table assertion of M31**, with ZisK as Goldilocks.
- **Governance economics is a genuine strength** (economist: 9.5 on chapter 8, 8.3 on chapters 2 and 13): ceremonies, the capex/opex frame, and verifier governance carry real analytical content rather than name-drops.
- **Assessment readiness is high overall** (assessor: 9.4 mean) — most chapters supply what a student needs to produce the specified evidence of mastery; chapter 12 is again the exception (6.0).
